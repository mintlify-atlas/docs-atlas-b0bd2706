The Biometric Patient ID System lets hospital staff register and identify patients using their fingerprints, captured directly from a mobile device camera. It eliminates manual ID checks, reduces patient misidentification risk, and integrates with your hospital's EHR and insurance systems upon a successful match.

The system is composed of three components working together: a **Flutter mobile app** for frontline nurses, a **Laravel REST API** that enforces role-based access and hospital scoping, and a **Python/FastAPI microservice** that runs the OpenCV-based fingerprint processing pipeline.

## How it works

The system uses **ORB (Oriented FAST and Rotated BRIEF)** feature extraction to convert a fingerprint image into a compact binary template. During verification, the probe template is compared against enrolled templates using a BFMatcher with Lowe's ratio test, producing a normalized match score. A score of **0.35 or above** is considered a positive match for hospital-wide identification.

<Note>
  All verification attempts are gated by a geofence check. The device must be within the hospital's configured GPS radius **and** connected to the authorized WiFi network before a scan is accepted.
</Note>

## Role model

Access to every endpoint is controlled by a four-level role matrix:

| Role | Primary responsibilities |
|------|--------------------------|
| `super_admin` | Cross-hospital management, no clinical data access |
| `admin` | Own-hospital staff and patient management, emergency enrollment |
| `doctor` | Read-only access to patient records, EHR, and verification logs |
| `nurse` | Patient registration, fingerprint enrollment, and verification scans |
